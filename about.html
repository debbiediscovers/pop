Pencils of Promise






​Like Adam Braun, Pencil of Promise’s founder, I believe that every child deserves an education. Building a school is something that I have always wanted to do, and it feels like the right time to do it.
Pencils of Promise (PoP)  was set up by Adam Braun, an ex-Bainee, who has built an extremely impressive  organisation which builds schools in four countries: Laos, Nicaragua, Ghana and Guatemala. I got to know about PoP because Bain offered to send all alums a copy of Adam’s best-selling book: “The Promise of a Pencil” in one of their alumni letters.  

I remember exactly which day I received my copy of the book because  I tweeted about it !

Why Pencils of Promise

POWER OF COLLECTIVE SMALL ACTS 
They believe in the power of collective, small acts of many. PoP was founded on the belief that even a small gesture, like handing a pencil to a child, is an important act and that every individual has something meaningful to contribute to the greater good.

100% FOR-PURPOSE
PoP is guided by the revolutionary “for-purpose” approach. Blending the head of a for-profit business with the heart of a humanitarian nonprofit, PoP rigorously measures the return on investment of every donor dollar spent. In typical Bain fashion, long-term sustainable results are what they're all about.

100% DIRECT GIVING
Donors rarely know where their money goes, so PoP set out to change this. By covering our operational costs through private donors, events and companies, 100% of every dollar donated online goes directly into our programs to educate more children.

100% LOCAL LEADERSHIP
All of their Country Directors are from the country they lead. 20% of costs are contributed by the local community in terms of building materials and transportation. 

100% SUCCESS RATE
Every school opened to date is fully operational and educating students daily. To date, over 200 teachers have been trained, 2,000 scholarships given out, over 300 schools built with over 33,000 students educated.  All in all, communities of over 300,000 people positively impacted.  

How can you help?

You can donate any amount but here are some examples of how you can help:

$0  - You can donate your voice and share this campaign  with your friends and ask them to support a good cause. 

USD $25 will provide a child access to education for one year.  (USD 25, SGD 35)

USD $100 will sponsor a water and health programme.  This includes building bathrooms and sanitary stations and educating children about proper hygiene.

USD $250 will sponsor a secondary school scholarship. Using historical data, high risks schools where students are most likely to drop out arae identified. Pencils of Promise offers scholarships which cover tuition, books, lodging and transportation. Students’ performance is also tracked, and a 75% attendance record must be maintained in order to continue receiving the scholarship. 

USD $250 will sponsor a mobile learning kit contains books, phonic games and creative educational tools for teachers or one e-reader provides a student with 100 books in both English and the local language (PoP is revolutionalizing technology in the classroom for developing countries.  More information can be found here)

USD $500 will provide teaching support. PoP teachers  that creates the educational materials required, identify and train the teachers, and track the educational progress of the student to evaluate the effectiveness of the programme.

USD $25,000 will build an entire school. One thing I love about Pencils of Promise is that they are results driven. And not just short-term results, achieving sustainable change is very important.  As a result, PoP chooses identifies communities that are deeply invested in their children’s education.
