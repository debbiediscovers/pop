Build a School with Me
I believe that every child deserves and education and building a school is something that I've always wanted to do.

So in Oct 2015, inspired by Adam Braun, an ex-Bainee who founded Pencils of Promise, a charity that builds schools in developing countries, I decided to start a fundraising campaign.

Instead of celebrating my birthday, I decided to ask my friends and family to help me build a school. I gave myself 12 months to raise US$25,000 and build one school.  

But I did it in 46 days with 80 donors of the kindest, most generous and closest friends and a surprising large number of strangers donating large amounts. 

Yet, a large proportion of my network was still untapped and a lot of friends had committed donations that had yet to be donated.  So I committed to raising another US$50,000 to build another two schools 

Over Christmas of 2015, I convinced six kids to give up their Christmas presents to donate to the campaign. I had a couple of rockstar kids on my team who made kick-ass videos which we posted on Facebook. What I learnt: Facebook can be an incredibly powerful tool.  All I did was to tag their parents and post it on their walls, and we raised $4,500 because some good hearted friend would invariably see it and donate.

And before I knew it, we had raised $75,000.

A few years later, three schools were build.
